Smart traffic signal optimisation

One AI signal brain
for every chowk.

ChowkPilot decides, every few seconds, which approach of a junction gets green, using only what a roadside camera can count. It was trained on synthetic junctions and two real Hyderabad areas and runs zero-shot on real Hyderabad junctions it has never seen, with Indian mixed traffic, lane-less two-wheelers, left-hand driving and an ambulance green corridor.

How it works

A learned generalisation of max-pressure control, the classical rule with a proven throughput-optimality guarantee.

1

Sense like a camera

Only what a roadside camera can measure: vehicles, stopped vehicles, occupancy and waiting time in the last 150 m of each lane, arrivals in the last 50 m, seconds since a vehicle last crossed the stop line, how full the exit roads are, and whether an ambulance is approaching.

2

Score every phase

Max-pressure scores a phase as Σ(in − out) over the movements it releases. ChowkPilot keeps that sum structure, takes the max-pressure score as a prior and learns a correction with deep reinforcement learning (Double DQN, semi-Markov rewards on true vehicle delay).

3

Stay safe and fair

A fixed safety layer enforces minimum and maximum green, a 120 s max-red guarantee, yellow and all-red clearance, whatever the policy outputs. An ambulance approaching within 150 m gets a green corridor.

4

Generalise

Trained on 11 synthetic layouts and 2 real areas with randomised demand, time-of-day profiles and camera noise. Tested on 3 real Hyderabad areas it never saw.

Simulation: Eclipse SUMO (EPL-2.0). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Demand is synthetic, calibrated to near-capacity peak conditions, not measured counts. ChowkPilot is a research prototype for decision support; real deployment needs local validation and traffic-police approval.
